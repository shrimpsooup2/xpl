how to play

you're a glossy blank figure with a glowing heart and nothing but your fists, pouring through dreamlike maps at 15 m/s. the one who moves best gets to the good guns first, so most of this page is about moving.

moving

every move keeps your speed or adds to it. nothing quietly eats it, and falling never hurts.

run

w a s d

quick to full speed, with a little weight to it. stairs and small lips never interrupt a run.

jump

space

always the same height. you can still jump just after running off a ledge, and a jump pressed just before you land goes off as you land.

air strafe

a / d + mouse

hold a or d and turn with the mouse to steer in the air and pick up a little speed, like quake.

slide

ctrl

at speed on the ground: a boost to start and low friction after, so a slide carries. downhill it speeds you up.

slide-hop

space

jump out of a slide and keep every bit of your speed. it's the move everything else chains through.

dash

shift

a burst the way you're moving that leaves you faster: a little on the ground, a lot in the air. at the top of a jump it carries you about twice as far. two charges.

smashdown

ctrl in the air

slam straight down. a shockwave where you land, then bounce back up (higher the further you fell) or slide out. best on someone's head.

wall ride

automatic

jump along a wall and you run on it for a moment, gravity easing off.

wall jump

space

on or near a wall, kick off it. three before you touch the ground again.

mantle

automatic

move into a ledge and you vault onto it, keeping most of your speed.

fighting

♥ the heartshot

the heart is the ring of pink beads in everyone's chest, a little off the middle. a shot through it from the sp-12, the marshal .357 or the heron .308 kills outright, whatever their health, from the front, back or side.

nothing else can: no pellets, no sprays, no fists, and never through a wall. aim for it when they're predictable: riding a wall, flying off a smashdown, standing still.

aiming

right click brings the gun up to your eye: each gun zooms by its own amount and tightens its spread, and aiming never slows you down. the heron's scope cuts to the scope view.

turning slows with the zoom (aim sensitivity in settings), and aiming can be a toggle instead of a hold.

getting hit

a red arc round the crosshair points at whoever hit you, wider for harder hits, and follows them as you turn. up is in front of you, down is behind.

fists

you always have them. a punch hits harder the faster you're going, and punching never slows you down.

throwing

q throws your gun, empty or not. it hurts whoever it hits and lands where anyone can pick it up again with what's left in it.

free-for-all and teams

free-for-all

short rounds, one life each, first to 5 rounds.

  • a new map every round, and a round lasts 70 s at most. last one standing takes it.
  • you spawn with your fists. guns float over pads round the map: walk over one empty-handed to take it, or press e to swap.
  • no reloads: what's in the gun is what you get. walking over the same kind of gun tops yours up.
  • an empty gun stays in your hands until you throw it or take another, and lasts until the next gun from its pad is taken.
  • health doesn't come back.

teams

four against four on one map. first team to 50 kills, or ahead after 10 minutes.

  • pick your gun with 1–6: in the countdown it's in your hands at once, while you're down it's yours next life. your pick is saved.
  • no guns on the map. ammo boxes stand all over it: walk into one and it tops your gun up by half a magazine, and it's back 10 s later.
  • health comes back 5 s after you were last hit. you're back 3 s after you die, at your team's end, away from the enemy.
  • kills within 4 s of each other chain: double killtriple killquad killpenta kill. kills without dying make a streak: on a roll at 3, heating up at 5, unstoppable at 8, untouchable at 12.
  • teams are told apart by hats: every hat is mostly your team's colour. no hat, and a triangle in your colour hangs over your head.

when you die

you fall apart. literally: the body turns out to have been diced all along, and it crumbles into a heap while a spotlight watches. after a heartshot the beads spill out across the floor.

then, until you're back, you watch someone who's still going: whoever got you first. click for the next person, right click for the last, esc for the menu.

settings

from the title screen or the menu (esc). everything takes effect at once and is saved as you change it; defaults puts a tab back.

controlssensitivity, aim sensitivity, invert look, toggle aim
keysevery action, two keys each: click a slot and press a key or mouse button
videowindow, vsync, frame cap, pixels (how tall the 3d picture is), colours (a 16-bit look)
camerafield of view, speed fov, camera motion, smoothing, screen shake, wall ride tilt, ui motion, landing dip, speed lines, impact frames
soundvolume, for when there are sounds

online

host

online → host a game: pick the style, the port (27960) and a password if you like. friends on your network join at the address the lobby shows; over the internet, forward the port or try open the port. add bots, then start.

join

online → join a game: the host's address (192.168.1.20, or example.com:27961 for another port) and the password if there is one.

know the risks

whoever hosts decides the game and can see your ip address. traffic isn't encrypted and there's no anti-cheat. play on hosts you trust, and host for people you know.
