movement-first arena fps

every round drops you into a new small, surreal map with a new spread of guns on the floor, and whoever moves best gets to the good ones first.

one life a round. rounds last seconds. a perfect shot to the heart ends it.

how it plays

momentum is sacred

run, slide, slide-hop, dash, wall ride, wall jump, mantle and smashdown. every move keeps your speed or adds to it, and nothing you do with a gun slows you down. a dash at the top of a jump carries you about twice as far.

slideslide-hopdashwall ridewall jumpmantlesmashdown

the heartshot

everyone has a heart: six pink beads going round in a pocket in their chest, like a loading spinner. put a shot from a precision gun through it and they're gone, whatever their health. it's small. that's the point.

♥ sp-12♥ marshal .357♥ heron .308

guns off the floor

in free-for-all you spawn with your fists and race for the guns on the pads. there are no reloads: what's in the gun is what you get. when it runs dry, throw it at someone's face and go and find the next one.

a new map every round

rounds last 15 to 40 seconds, and every one is on a different map, so no two play the same. read the map in the countdown, plan a route, go.

alone or together

practise against bots in either style, host a game from the menu, or run a dedicated server. the server decides everything; your own movement is predicted so it feels instant, and everyone else is smoothed out.

get it

not out yet

xtrapartial is in pre-production. the movement, the guns, both ways to play and online play are in, on greybox maps. you can run it from the source now:

  1. install godot 4.7.2, the standard build (not .net).
  2. get the source from github.
  3. in godot choose import, pick project.godot, and press f5.
source on github design doc

run a server

no window, no player, games back to back while anyone's connected:

godot --headless --path . -- --server \
  --mode teams --maps boulevard,depot

or host from the menu: online → host a game, and friends join at the address the lobby shows. only play on hosts you trust: whoever runs the server decides the game.
